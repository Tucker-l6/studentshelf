Rental and Damage Policy

During the beta, rentals are agreements between two students: the Shelf doesn't hold money, collect deposits, or cover damage. Agree on the price, deposit, and return date in the chat before pickup, so there's a written record.

How a rental works

  1. Request. The renter picks a pickup day. Rentals last up to 3 days unless both students agree otherwise in the chat.
  2. Confirm. The lender accepts or declines. Once accepted, the renter gets a pickup code.
  3. Pick up. Meet at the listing's campus spot. Both students check the item and take photos. The lender enters the renter's code.
  4. Return. The renter brings the item back to the same spot by the due date and marks it returned.
  5. Close. The lender checks the item within 24 hours and confirms it came back fine or reports damage.

Lenders

  • List only items you own, that are clean and in the condition shown in your photos.
  • State the item's retail value honestly. It sets the suggested deposit and caps what a renter can owe.
  • You may ask for a deposit, paid directly to you. The app suggests about half the retail value. Return it within 24 hours of a good-condition return.
  • Don't list items worn directly against the skin, like swimwear or underwear.

Renters

  • Use the item for its normal purpose. Don't alter, tailor, or lend it to anyone else.
  • Follow the care notes. Don't wash or dry-clean it unless the lender agrees.
  • Return it on time, in the condition you received it, apart from normal light wear.
  • Tell the lender right away if something happens to it.

Late returns

Unless you agree otherwise, a late return costs $5 per day, taken from the deposit. After 7 days late, the lender can treat the item as lost.

Damage and loss

These are the defaults when students haven't agreed to something else. The renter never owes more than the retail value stated in the listing.

What happened What the renter owes the lender
Normal light wear (small creases, a loose thread) Nothing
Needs professional cleaning (a stain, a strong smell) The cleaning cost, with a receipt
Repairable damage (a small tear, a broken zipper, a lost button) The repair cost, with a receipt
Ruined or lost A fair replacement value: what the item would cost in similar condition, up to the stated retail value

The lender can keep the deposit up to what's owed and must return the rest with an itemized explanation and photos.

  1. Talk it through in the chat and share photos from pickup and return.

Safety and Meetup Guidelines

Meet in a busy public spot on campus, check the item before any money moves, and leave if anything feels off.

Where and when to meet

  • Use the pickup spots in the app: Horn Library, Reynolds Campus Center, Trim Dining Hall, Webster Center, or Weissman Foundry.
  • Meet when the building is open and busy. Avoid late nights.
  • Don't meet in dorm rooms, parking lots, or off campus. For something too big to carry, like a fridge, meet outside the building and bring a friend to help.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting.

At the handoff

  1. Check that the person matches the name and class year in the app.
  2. Inspect the item: turn it on, check sizes, look for damage. For rentals, both take photos.
  3. Pay only after you've seen the item and you're happy with it.
  4. Show your handoff code last, once the item is in your hands.

Paying safely during the beta

  • Pay in person, with cash or a payment app, at the meetup.
  • Don't send money before meeting. The only exception is a rental deposit you've agreed to in the chat.
  • On payment apps, send only to the person you're meeting, and double-check the username.
  • Never pay with gift cards, and never accept an "overpayment" with a request to send the difference back.

Red flags

  • Asking to meet off campus, at night, or somewhere private
  • Pushing you to pay first, pay extra, or use gift cards
  • Asking for your handoff code before you have the item
  • A price far below what similar items sell for, or photos that look copied from a store
  • Pressure to move the conversation off the Shelf right away

If something goes wrong

  • Danger or an emergency: call 911, or contact Babson Public Safety.
  • A scam, no-show, or bad behavior: use Report in the app or email tlindley1@babson.edu. Include screenshots.
  • A trade or rental dispute: follow the steps in the Rental and Damage Policy.
